### Any other program
- Look for a command-line mode first (`--help` through `run_program`, output is captured): many programs convert, export or batch-process without the GUI.
- Otherwise: `open_app`, `wait`, `screenshot`, then menus by keyboard (`alt` or the menu's access keys) or clicks on what you can read in the screenshot. Confirm every dialog by reading it.
- Unknown dialogs about licenses, updates, telemetry or accounts: dismiss with the safest choice ("Not now", "Cancel", "Close"); never install or sign in.
