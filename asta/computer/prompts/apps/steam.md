### Steam / Lutris
- Installed Steam and Lutris games appear in `list_games`; launch with `launch_game` using the id. Steam must be installed and signed in; if a login window appears, stop and tell the user (never type a password).
- Installing, buying, updating or uninstalling games is not part of "opening a game": do not do it unless the request says so.
- Big games take 10–60 s to show a window: `wait`, then screenshot.
